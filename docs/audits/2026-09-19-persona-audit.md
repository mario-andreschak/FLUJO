# Persona audit and implementation plan — 2026-09-19

## Intended outcome

The goal in [#505](https://github.com/mario-andreschak/FLUJO/issues/505) is an editable, persistent AI teammate that takes ownership of an objective, maintains executable work, uses its configured capabilities, verifies outcomes, recovers from transient failures and restarts, and continues until success or an explicit owner control. The Frederik / Marketing Agent example is the acceptance scenario. A role name does not confer tools or account access. A finite activity, simulated throughput, and public-world success are different claims.

[#418](https://github.com/mario-andreschak/FLUJO/issues/418) defines the product: a Persona combines a Role, normal editable Core and Behavior Flows, Apps, and native Memory. [#435](https://github.com/mario-andreschak/FLUJO/issues/435) requires the complete beginner journey, migration, accessibility, translations, privacy, and workspace isolation. [#448](https://github.com/mario-andreschak/FLUJO/issues/448) and [#489](https://github.com/mario-andreschak/FLUJO/issues/489) require measured bounded-state, memory, learning/rollback, recovery and exact-release acceptance evidence.
