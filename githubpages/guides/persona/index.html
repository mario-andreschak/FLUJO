Before the first session

A helper with a little continuity.

A Persona has a name, instructions, memory, and goals that carry across conversations. You can review what it remembers and how its work is progressing.

  1. Start with the working AI connection from the first assistant guide.
  2. In Settings, turn on Enable experimental features. Open More → Personas → Create Persona.
  3. Name her Maya. Give her a clear purpose: help prepare a beginner coffee workshop while separating confirmed decisions from suggestions.
  4. Choose a Role, or Create a simple Role. The role supplies the underlying agent and model. If you see Configure models, finish that setup before continuing.
  5. Choose only the apps and additional behaviors needed for this task. Review the setup, then create Maya.

Use Memory → Add memory after creation for facts you want to keep. The creation wizard does not add those memories for you.

Day 1 · Compressed session

Give her a clear finish line.

Open Give this Persona a goal. We started with a 90-minute workshop for six adults at three shared stations. The goal asks for a plan, invitation draft, shopping list, and run sheet, with the venue and date to be confirmed before finishing.

Enter a short title in Goal, then put the details in Helpful context. Include the public guide and your reviewed checklist from the first assistant. Reusing its connections does not transfer its chat into Maya’s context. Turn on Keep working on this goal deliberately, and use the success criteria to say that Maya should wait for your final approval.

Take it into your own work

A continuing conversation.
A visible next step.

Try a project with a clear finish line and a few decisions worth keeping. Give the persona a specific role, tell it what “done” means, and check the work as the plan changes.

What about ongoing and scheduled work?

Keep working on this goal enables ongoing work. Review its timing and stop controls deliberately; the “Day” labels in this guide do not create a schedule. FLUJO’s server must stay running, and a private workspace may need unlocking after startup.

Provider limits, failed tools, and approval requests can interrupt work. Inspect activity and outputs, and use pause or stop when needed. Personas, Roles, and persistent goals remain experimental. Read the maturity notes.

Keep the boundary clear. This workshop example drafts an invitation and shopping list; it does not send messages or buy anything. Model calls and scheduled work use your provider’s access and allowance. Data and costs.
