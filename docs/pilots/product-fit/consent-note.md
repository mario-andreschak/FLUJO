## What the pilot is

You install FLUJO on your own computer, follow the [tester guide](tester-guide.md),
and try it on a task you already do. Once a week you answer a short form. We want
to learn whether FLUJO is useful again and again, and where it fails.

## What we collect

- Your weekly form answers: did you use FLUJO, which tasks finished or failed,
  whether it helped, and what went wrong.
- How long your first run took, and whether you needed help.
- Only if you choose to: a **diagnostic report** that FLUJO creates on your
  computer (Settings → Privacy & usage → Diagnostic report). It holds the FLUJO
  and Node.js versions, your operating system, how many AI connections, apps,
  agents and automations you have, and how many runs and errors happened in
  the last seven days. You can read every line before you share it.

## What we never collect

- API keys, passwords or tokens.
- Your prompts, conversations or AI answers.
- File names, folder paths, web addresses, server names or agent names.
- Workspace exports, backups or logs.
- Screenshots, unless you offer a specific one and say it may be used.

Please do not send any of these. If you do by mistake, we delete them and tell
you.

FLUJO does not send the diagnostic report or your form anywhere. You decide
what to send, and to whom.

## Things to know before you start

- FLUJO runs on your computer. Tool servers you connect (MCP) run with your
  user's permissions and can do what that program is built to do, such as read
  files. Give them only the folders and accounts you are comfortable with.
- If you use a cloud AI provider, what you send to the AI goes to that
  provider and may cost money under your own account. A free local model
  (Ollama) keeps everything on your machine.
- FLUJO also has an anonymous daily "I'm active" signal, which you can turn off
  in Settings → Privacy & usage. It is separate from this pilot.
