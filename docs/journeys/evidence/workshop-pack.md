## Workshop plan

- **Participants:** Eight adults at beginner level, including one choosing decaf.
- **Venue:** Community Room.
- **Date and time:** Saturday, October 17, 2026, 10:00–11:30 AM.
- **Setup:** Three shared stations, with two or three participants at each. An extra set of lead equipment is optional; the demonstration can use a shared station.
- **Topics:** Scales, kettles, grind size, coffee-to-water ratio, the bloom, pouring technique and tasting adjustments.
- **Proposed starting recipe:** 20 g coffee to 300 g water, a medium grind and water around 93°C. These are the generated workshop's suggested starting settings.
- **Decaf:** Clearly label the decaf beans, keep them separate from regular coffee, and use a rinsed brewer for the decaf participant.
